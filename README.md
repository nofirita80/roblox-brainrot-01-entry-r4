Steal a Snackrot — a steal-a-brainrot style game built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best AI Roblox brainrot game" bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## How to play

Snack-brainrot characters ride the central conveyor around the arena. Click one to claim it into your base (it costs Cash, and your base needs a free slot). While a character sits in your base it earns Cash for you every second — rarer snacks earn far more.

Walk into another player's base, click one of their characters, and carry it home: click a free slot in your own base to drop it there and it becomes yours. The owner can fight back with the Slap tool — a good slap knocks the thief back and makes them drop what they carry. Lock your base from its red lock button to keep thieves out for 60 seconds.

Spend Cash in the shop: a Spawn Boost makes the next conveyor character Epic or better, and a Speed Coil makes you run 50% faster for a minute. Press E to drop whatever you are carrying.

## The roster

Ten snack characters across five rarities, from the $100 Choco Croissant ($2/s) to the $500,000 Candy King ($10,000/s). Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic snacks all spawn on the conveyor by weight — the Mythic ones are rare for a reason.

## Run it

```sh
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl
```

Open `game.rbxl` in Roblox Studio and publish it as your experience. The play code for this entry is `makefun-mule-hemlock-wallaby` — it is in the experience description on Roblox, and the place and universe ids live in `PLAY.json`.
